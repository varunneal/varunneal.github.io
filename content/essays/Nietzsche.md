Works of Nietzsche should be found in [[Preface to the Library |Library]]. Therefore, in this section I am just compiing some of my favorite quotes by him and about him. 

I am somewhat cautious to quote Nietzsche for the same reason he is so quotable: he speaks in pithy, contradicting aphorisms. If you take any of his decrees too seriously you will be made a fool by his next sentence... despite this I reject the notion that Nietzsche is uninterpretable. Nietzsche did not obfuscate his thought; above all he prides himself on his clarity.
